IMPOSSIBLE

Privacy Policy · Effective 26 October 2026

IMPOSSIBLE (“the App”) is published by Lounge Lizard. This policy describes how the App handles information.

Summary

The App stores level progress and settings on your device only. The 1.0.0 build does not require an account and does not operate a game backend that receives your progress.

Data we store on device

  • Cleared level identifiers and unlock progress
  • Optional settings (reduced motion, ghost seams)
  • Last successful yaw per cleared level (for your own replay reference)

This data remains in on-device storage (for example localStorage / app preferences).

Network

Opening this privacy page or future store links may load over the network. Gameplay itself does not require a network connection.

Children

The App is a general audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the Lounge Lizard contact email listed on the Google Play store listing for IMPOSSIBLE.

Changes

We may update this policy when the App’s data practices change. The effective date above will be revised accordingly.
